"This report is an evidence and risk-assessment layer and does not constitute legal or regulatory certification."

1. Tax Registration (12A / 80G validity).

5. Regulatory watchlist, litigation, or adverse media screening.

Status Definitions:
- VerificationStatus: VERIFIED, PARTIALLY_VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED, MISSING, FLAGGED.
- DueDiligenceRisk: LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, CRITICAL, UNKNOWN.

- "report_id": Identifier with prefix DD-XXXX.

  - "check_name": Name of specific check.
  - "status": VerificationStatus.

  - "evidence": Specific citation or verified fact.
  - "confidence": Float between 0.0 and 1.0.
  - "checked_at": ISO timestamp string.
- "flags": Array of identified risk flags or warnings.
- "missing_documents": Array of unsubmitted mandatory compliance documents.
- "model_name": Model name.
- "model_version": "due-diligence-v1".
- "disclaimer": "This report is an evidence and risk-assessment layer and does not constitute legal or regulatory certification."

NGO DATA INPUT:
{ngo_payload}

Respond strictly with valid JSON conforming to the DueDiligenceReport schema.
