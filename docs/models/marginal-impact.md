# Marginal Impact Engine Model Specification

## 1. Purpose
The Marginal Impact Engine represents the core intellectual differentiator of AllocateAI. Traditional CSR portfolio selection relies on static total scores or historical prestige, favoring established programs that may have already reached their scale limits. The Marginal Impact Engine answers the critical operational question: *"What additional measurable social impact will be generated by allocating the next rupee (or ₹1 lakh increment) to this project?"*

In Phase 4, the `MarginalImpactEngine` (`marginal-v1`) provides deterministic calculation of incremental impact, elasticity under diminishing returns, and simulation capabilities across standard allocation tiers (₹1L, ₹2L, ₹5L, ₹10L).

## 2. Inputs
The model operates on structured project performance indicators:
* **`Project` Entity**:
  * `financials.current_funding_paise`: Baseline budget in integer paise ($ge 0$).
  * `financials.requested_amount_paise`: Total planned capital capacity in integer paise.
  * `impact_dna.estimated_impact_per_lakh`: Expected baseline impact output per ₹1 Lakh.
  * `impact_dna.cost_efficiency_score`: Baseline efficiency score ($\in [0.0, 1.0]$).
  * `impact_dna.need_score`: Need severity index ($\in [0.0, 1.0]$).
* **`SaturationResult` (Optional/Recommended)**:
  * `saturation_index`: Regional capital saturation ($\in [0.0, 1.0]$).
* **Incremental Step (`increment_paise`)**:
  * Default: `10,000,000` paise = ₹1 Lakh ($> 0$).

## 3. Outputs
The model emits a validated `MarginalImpactResult` conforming to Technical Contract v1.0:
* **`marginal_impact_score`**: Normalized rate of incremental return $\in [0.0, 1.0]$.
* **`diminishing_return_factor`**: Elasticity factor $\in [0.0, 1.0]$ capturing absorptive capacity and saturation.
* **`baseline_impact`**: Projected cumulative outcome volume at baseline budget.
* **`projected_impact`**: Projected cumulative outcome volume after adding the increment.
* **`incremental_impact`**: Absolute gain in outcome volume (`projected_impact - baseline_impact`).
* **`impact_per_lakh`**: Normalized efficiency metric (incremental output units per ₹1 Lakh deployed).
* **`calculation_version`**: `"marginal-v1"`.
* **`component_breakdown`**: Detailed explainability dictionary containing:
  * `baseline_impact`, `projected_impact`, `incremental_impact`, `impact_per_lakh`
  * `diminishing_return_factor`, `saturation_penalty`, `need_bonus`
* **`weights_used`**:
  * `cost_efficiency_weight`: `0.40`
  * `need_bonus_weight`: `0.35`
  * `saturation_penalty_weight`: `0.25`
* **`allocation_context`**: Structural budget context containing:
  * `baseline_budget_lakh`, `projected_budget_lakh`, `increment_lakh`, `budget_ratio`

## 4. Version

* **Input Schema**: `dna-v1`

## 5. Assumptions
* Projects exhibit non-linear marginal returns (diminishing marginal utility as funding scales past absorptive capacity).
* All budgetary comparisons are computed strictly in integer paise ($10,000,000\text{ paise} = \text{₹1 Lakh}$).
* The default demonstration increment step is ₹1 Lakh (`10,000,000` paise).
* Calculations remain strictly deterministic, containing no stochastic simulation or unseeded randomness.
* No system clocks or temporal dependencies in calculation routines.

## 6. Future Formula Section
### Formula Implementation (marginal-v1)

#### 1. Diminishing Return Factor ($\eta \in [0.0, 1.0]$)
$$\text{budget\_ratio} = \frac{\text{projected\_budget\_paise}}{\text{requested\_amount\_paise}}$$
$$\text{base\_decay} = \frac{1.0}{1.0 + 0.50 \cdot \text{budget\_ratio}}$$
$$\text{saturation\_factor} = 1.0 - 0.35 \cdot \text{saturation\_index}$$
$$\text{need\_factor} = 0.80 + 0.20 \cdot \text{need\_score}$$
$$\text{diminishing\_return\_factor} = \text{clip}\big(\text{base\_decay} \cdot \text{saturation\_factor} \cdot \text{need\_factor},\, 0.0,\, 1.0\big)$$

#### 2. Incremental Impact Quantities
$$\text{effective\_rate\_per\_lakh} = \text{estimated\_impact\_per\_lakh} \times \text{diminishing\_return\_factor}$$
$$\text{incremental\_impact} = \text{effective\_rate\_per\_lakh} \times \left(\frac{\text{increment\_paise}}{\text{PAISE\_PER\_LAKH}}\right)$$
$$\text{projected\_impact} = \text{baseline\_impact} + \text{incremental\_impact}$$

#### 3. Calibrated Marginal Impact Score
$$\text{effective\_potential} = 0.40 \cdot \text{cost\_efficiency\_score} + 0.35 \cdot \text{need\_score} + 0.25 \cdot (1.0 - \text{saturation\_index})$$
$$\text{marginal\_impact\_score} = \text{round}\Big(\text{clip}\big(\text{diminishing\_return\_factor} \cdot \text{effective\_potential},\, 0.0,\, 1.0\big),\, 6\Big)$$

### Increment Simulation Tiers
Supported via `simulate_increment()`:
* **1L**: `10,000,000` paise (₹1 Lakh)
* **2L**: `20,000,000` paise (₹2 Lakh)
* **5L**: `50,000,000` paise (₹5 Lakh)
* **10L**: `100,000,000` paise (₹10 Lakh)
